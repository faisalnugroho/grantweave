KNOW WHAT THE RECORD MEANS

Deliberately bounded. Openly inspectable.

Only earlier RESERVED proposals in the same round are compared. This is not a global duplicate-funding detector. Submission order takes priority; the round creator decides when to seal submissions. Public submissions can fill the eight available slots.

Model judgments can be wrong. Missing evidence, commitment mismatch or uncertain findings cannot reserve credits. Terminal decisions have no appeal in v1; use a new round for a revised assessment. This prototype never pays out funds.
